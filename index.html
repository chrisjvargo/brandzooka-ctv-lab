Read the numbers honestly

  • Completion = share of impressions watched to the end (0–100).
  • CTR = clicks ÷ impressions, on a 0–100 scale here.
  • Viewability = share counted as viewable (0–100). A measured view is not attention — it only means the ad was on screen long enough to count.
  • Delivery = actual impressions ÷ the platform's projection. Over 100 means it over-delivered.
  • Win rate = share of auctions this bidder won. Higher usually means less picky bidding.

About this data

  • 115 self-serve video creatives in 148 flights, Jan–Sep 2017, from the Brandzooka platform. One tile = one creative; multi-flight tiles show honest aggregates (counts summed, CTR recomputed from totals, completion/viewability/win rate impression-weighted).
  • Some audience values are hashed codes (e.g. place IDs). They prove targeting existed but can't be read — the page labels them instead of guessing.
  • Daypart grids show active hours across a 7-day flight week. Weekday mapping is unverified, so days are numbered, not named.
  • Landing-page links are 2017 originals. Many are dead; they still show what the click was selling.
